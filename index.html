Niki & Her Privacy Policy - Niki and Her

Privacy Policy for Niki and Her

Last Updated: 24 November 2026

1. Overview

Niki and Her ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy—especially when it comes to sensitive health and wellness information. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

2. Local-Only Data Storage (No External Servers)

All menopause tracking data, symptoms, cycle logs, notes, and personal preferences you enter into Niki and Her are stored locally on your device only.

  • No Cloud Storage: We do not collect, transmit, store, or have access to your health or tracking data on any external server or cloud database.
  • No Account Required: We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or phone number to use the app.

3. Health & Wellness Disclaimer

The educational content and tracking tools in Niki and Her are for informational and self-tracking purposes only and do not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional regarding medical questions or symptoms.

4. Third-Party Services & Analytics

Niki and Her does not use third-party advertising networks, data brokers, or cross-app tracking.

Note: If you enable Apple's standard opt-in crash diagnostics on your device, Apple may provide anonymised, aggregated crash logs to help us fix technical bugs. These logs cannot be used to identify you.

5. Data Control, Backups & Deletion

Because your data lives exclusively on your iPhone or iPad, you have complete control over it:

  • Viewing & Editing: You can view, edit, or delete individual entries directly inside the app at any time.
  • Complete Deletion: You can permanently erase all app data by clearing your data in the app's settings or by uninstalling Niki and Her from your device.
  • Device Backups: If you use Apple iCloud Backup or encrypted Finder/iTunes backups for your device, your local app data may be included in those personal backups under your own Apple ID security settings. We do not have access to your backups.

6. Children’s Privacy

Niki and Her is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the UK and EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your Rights (UK GDPR & International Users)

Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and similar international privacy laws, you have rights regarding your personal data. Because Niki and Her stores all tracking data locally on your device and does not collect or hold your personal or health data on our servers, we do not act as a data controller for your health logs—you maintain full, direct control over your information at all times.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page and within the app.

9. Support & Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or need technical support with Niki and Her, please contact us:

  • Email: Niki-and-Her@proton.me
  • Developer: Niki / Niki and Her

Note: If you contact us via email for support, we will only use your email address and the details you provide to respond to your enquiry. Support emails are not linked to any in-app activity.
